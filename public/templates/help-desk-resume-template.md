**CERTIFICATIONS**

**EDUCATION**

## The rules this skeleton bakes in
- **Scope line before bullets** — user count, volume, tier: your level in twelve seconds, without inflating your title.
- **Name the actual system**, not the category — "ServiceNow," not "ticketing software."
- **Approximations marked "~" are honest**; precise percentages you never saw are not.
- **One page** under ~8 years. Single column, no skill-rating graphics — plain text is what gets searched.

## The interview test
Every line should survive: "Tell me about that." If you'd stumble, rewrite it until you wouldn't.
