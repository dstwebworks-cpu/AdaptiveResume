# Clearance Line Cheat-Sheet
### A free one-pager from AdaptiveResume

## The format line (copy this shape)
**Status + Level — Granting agency, investigation or eligibility date**

- Active Secret clearance — DoD, eligibility granted [month/year]
- Active Top Secret/SCI — DoD, investigation completed [year]; enrolled in Continuous Vetting
- Secret eligibility current — last held access [month/year] at [employer]
- Interim Secret granted [month/year]; final adjudication pending

Why each piece: status (active or eligibility current) · level exactly as your record reads · the agency that granted it · the one date a security officer will check.

## Where the line goes
| Situation | Placement |
|-----------|-----------|
| Clearance is required for the job | Directly under your name and contact block |
| You also hold licenses or certifications | First line of a **Clearance & Credentials** section, right after your summary |
| Federal application through USAJOBS | Page one, always (two-page cap since the September 2025 OPM change) |

Never: buried in a skills list, or inside a job description on page two.

## The honest phrasings
| Your situation | Write it as |
|----------------|-------------|
| In a cleared job today | "Active Secret clearance — DoD, eligibility granted [month/year]" |
| Left the cleared job, eligibility still current | "Secret eligibility current — last held access [month/year] at [employer]" |
| Interim granted, final pending | "Interim Secret granted [month/year]; final adjudication pending" |
| Polygraph taken | Add only the type you took: "with CI polygraph ([year])" or "with full-scope polygraph ([year])" |
| DOE work | "DOE Q access authorization — [site], [year]" (not "Top Secret") |
| Public Trust position | "Public Trust (moderate risk) — [agency], [year]" under its own label — it is not a clearance |
| Posting says "must be able to obtain a clearance" | State the real requirement you meet: "U.S. citizen." Never "clearable." |
| Not sure of a date | Ask your FSO (facility security officer) before you write one |

## Three words, three meanings
- **Eligibility** — the government's adjudicated decision that you may be granted access at a level.
- **Access** — being read in for a specific job. Ends when you leave that job.
- **Active** — holding access right now.

## Terms recruiters search for (use the exact words)
Secret · Top Secret · TS/SCI and Top Secret/SCI (write both) · clearance · active · DoD · CI polygraph or full-scope polygraph (only if held) · Q clearance / L clearance (DOE) · Public Trust (own label) · T3 / T5 (only if your record shows it) · eligibility · Continuous Vetting

## Never on the page
- Classified program names, code words, or compartments
- Project details from a classified environment — describe the skill, not the mission
- Customer or contract names when the contract itself is classified
- Anything from your SF-86 (foreign contacts, finances, travel history)
- "SCI clearance" on its own (SCI is access on top of Top Secret, not a level)
- "Public Trust clearance" or "clearable"

## The test for every line
Could your FSO confirm it tomorrow? If yes, it belongs. If you would have to explain, rewrite it until the explanation is on the page.
