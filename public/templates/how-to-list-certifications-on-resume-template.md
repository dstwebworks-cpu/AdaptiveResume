# Certification Section Cheat-Sheet
### A free one-pager from AdaptiveResume

## The format line (copy this shape)
**Full name (Acronym) — Issuer, date earned or current-through**

- Certified ScrumMaster (CSM) — Scrum Alliance, earned 2025
- CompTIA Security+ — CompTIA, current through 05/2028
- OSHA 30-Hour General Industry — completed 2024

Why each piece: full name AND acronym (you don't know which gets searched) · issuer (what makes it checkable) · date (answers the currency question before it's asked).

## Where the section goes
| Situation | Placement |
|-----------|-----------|
| Cert is required for the job | High — right after your summary |
| Cert is supporting evidence | After Experience / Education |
| Field puts it in job titles (RN, PE, CPA, PMP) | Also after your name |

## What order
1. **Relevance first** — the credential the posting asks for leads, even if it's your oldest.
2. **Then recency** — newest first among equals.
3. Three relevant lines beat eight mixed ones.

## The honest phrasings
| Status | Write it as |
|--------|-------------|
| Earned, current | "— Issuer, current through [date]" |
| Earned, no expiry | "— Issuer, earned [year]" |
| Exam booked | "— exam scheduled [month/year]" |
| Partway through | "— in progress, expected [month/year]" |
| Expired, still relevant | "— Issuer (expired [year])" |
| Not started, no date | Leave it off |

## License vs. certification
License = government permission to practice (state nursing license, CDL, electrician). Certification = professional-body credential. Both? Heading: **Licenses & Certifications** — license first, with state and status: "Registered Nurse — Georgia, active."

## Never
- Bare acronym that reads as earned when it isn't
- "Certified in..." with no credential name or issuer
- An expired cert listed as current
- Course completions dressed up as certifications — list them under Training instead

## The test for every line
Could you hand over proof of it tomorrow? If yes, it belongs. If you'd have to explain, rewrite it until the explanation is on the page.
