# Blind Review Checklist for Hiring Teams

One page for deciding what a masked review will hide, what it will not, and what the record must hold before the review starts. This is a process checklist, not legal advice; the federal Uniform Guidelines on Employee Selection Procedures (29 CFR part 1607) and state and local rules still apply to any selection step.

## 1. Before the review: the record

- [ ] Every candidate record was built from the candidate's actual work, not a template.
- [ ] Every line was confirmed by the candidate or by a reviewer working from documents.
- [ ] Credentials were asked about and confirmed, never assumed from a title or a keyword.
- [ ] Records are in a consistent format: single column, standard headings, dates present.
- [ ] Thin records were built out before review; a masked review of a thin record is a coin flip.

## 2. What the mask will hide

- [ ] Name
- [ ] Photo
- [ ] Email address and phone number
- [ ] Street address
- [ ] School names (decide; hiding them removes one proxy and one signal)
- [ ] Graduation years (decide; the same trade-off)

## 3. What the mask will not hide, and how you will handle it

- [ ] Employer names and industries (a proxy; decide whether reviewers score the work or the brand)
- [ ] Years of experience and dates (a fact the interview will reveal; keep them)
- [ ] Vocabulary and writing quality (a signal about the record, not the candidate; a confirmed record equalizes it)
- [ ] The interview and reference steps are not blind; decide what the masked step is allowed to decide

## 4. During the review

- [ ] Reviewers decide on each line, not on the whole record at once.
- [ ] Rejections state which requirement was not met.
- [ ] Credential holds go to a second reviewer or to the candidate for confirmation.
- [ ] Every decision is recorded with the reviewer and the date.

## 5. After the review

- [ ] A per-candidate audit report exists showing each line's journey and the sign-off.
- [ ] Advancement rates are checked by group at the masked step and at the unmasked steps.
- [ ] Identity is restored only at the stage your process defines, and that stage is written down.
